## Purpose
Expose a simple, reproducible temporal objective (e.g., sequence prediction) with a clear scalar metric, fixed seeds, and a masking interface so cell-level counterfactual utility and whole-system performance are measurable.

## Why it is fundamental
Without a shared task interface, emergence, pruning, and compactness cannot be validated—only asserted. The harness is the empirical ground: same protocols for training loops, ablations, and later biological-substrate comparisons.
