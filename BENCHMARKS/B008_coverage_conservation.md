## Purpose
For inputs dominated by a soon-to-be-pruned cell, measure post-absorption ownership: surviving cells assume those patterns, and task error on that subset does not spike beyond a defined bound relative to pre-prune performance.

## What integrity looks like
Pruning that orphans input regions is footprint theater. Conservation of coverage links removal to continued function on the same stimuli.
