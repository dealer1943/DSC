## Purpose
Compare selection under full utility versus task-only utility: population diversity (effective number of types / activation decorrelation), coverage entropy, and footprint. Counterfactual (masking) marginals should disagree with naive correlation attributions on held-out probes.

## What integrity looks like
Task-only scores reliably produce monopoly states. A passing result shows coverage and novelty terms change selection outcomes in the direction of partitioned, non-redundant competence—not merely renamed loss weights.
