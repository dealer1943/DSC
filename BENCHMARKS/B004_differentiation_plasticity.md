## Purpose
Measure that sustained high utility increases differentiation toward commitment, and that sustained utility drop increases plasticity (movement toward STEM), including recovery of re-differentiation after a controlled utility collapse.

## What integrity looks like
Commitment without reversibility freezes early winners; differentiation that ignores utility is cosmetic. This benchmark couples the plasticity variable to measured usefulness over time.
